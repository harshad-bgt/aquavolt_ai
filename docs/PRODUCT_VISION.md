# Product Vision

## Long-Term Vision
AquaVolt AI aims to be an AI-Powered Water + Energy Management System for Agriculture. It is designed around the relationship between:
- Water
- Energy
- Borewell
- Crop
- Weather
- Solar

## Recommendation System (Future)
The future recommendation system will answer:
- "Should I run my pump now?"
- "When should I run the pump?"
- "Approximately how long should it run?"
- "What is the expected water and energy requirement?"
- "How is the borewell behaving and how long may recovery take?"
- "Why is AquaVolt making this recommendation?"

## Borewell Concept (Future)
AquaVolt will develop a "Borewell Behavior Profile" using pumping behavior, flow behavior, water-level behavior, and recovery cycles. It learns farm-specific behavior.

*Note: These advanced systems and concepts are FUTURE phases and are not implemented in the current development phase.*
